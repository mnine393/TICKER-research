# JPM owner memo

**Conclusion: watch the business.**

The return-on-equity record does not clear the stated standard in every reported fiscal year: ROE was 16.4%, 12.9%, 15.1%, 17.0%, and 15.7%. The every-year ROE check therefore fails.

The owner-earnings judgment cannot be made from this record. Capital spending is not found in every reported fiscal year, so owner earnings are not found and both owner-earnings checks are not known. Without those inputs, this memo cannot judge whether owner earnings were positive throughout the record or grew from the first fiscal year to the last. Operating cash flow also changed from $78,084.0 million and $107,119.0 million to $12,974.0 million, $-42,012.0 million, and $-147,782.0 million; that is a figure an owner should investigate, not replace with an estimate. This memo judges the business, not its share price.
