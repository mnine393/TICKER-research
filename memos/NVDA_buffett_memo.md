# NVDA owner memo

**Conclusion: own the business.**

The five-year 10-K record shows returns on shareholder capital above the required level in every year: ROE was 36.6%, 19.8%, 69.2%, 91.9%, and 76.3%. Owner earnings were positive in every year and grew from $8,132.0 million in the first reported year to $96,676.0 million in the last. The three owner-oriented checks all pass, for a score of 3 of 3.

The path was not perfectly smooth: owner earnings fell to $3,808.0 million in the second year before rising to $27,021.0 million, $60,853.0 million, and $96,676.0 million. Based only on this five-year SEC record, the business merits ownership; an owner should continue to watch whether that cash generation and return on equity remain durable. This memo judges the business, not its share price.
