# COST owner memo

**Conclusion: own the business.**

The SEC record shows a business that converts substantial cash into owner earnings while earning more than the required return on shareholder capital in every reported fiscal year. ROE was 28.3%, 25.1%, 31.2%, 27.8%, and 25.8%; the ROE check passes. Owner earnings were positive in every reported fiscal year and rose from $3,501.0 million to $9,382.0 million, so both owner-earnings checks pass.

The path was not perfectly smooth: owner earnings moved from $6,745.0 million to $6,629.0 million before reaching $7,837.0 million and then $9,382.0 million. An owner should continue to watch whether operating cash flow remains sufficient after capital spending, which rose from $3,891.0 million to $6,435.0 million. Based only on this SEC record, the business merits ownership; this memo judges the business, not its share price.


## My decision

I trust Buffett's verdict on COST (own the business), because every number came from Costco's 10-K through code, all three checks passed, and the app found no number the script did not print. It only judges five years of business quality, not the price.
